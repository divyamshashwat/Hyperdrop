# Nearby calibration sweep — people talking (simulated)

Run: `npm run sweep:nearby -- --trials 40` (2026-11-01, 4 cores, 341 s). 720 cells × 40 trials = 28,800 decodes
through the real `ToneDecoder`, plus 48 × 120 s of speech-only audio for false positives.

**Setup.** One packet at a random offset into synthetic speech (`scripts/nearby-sim/speech.ts`: gliding-pitch formant
vowels, s / sh / f fricatives, plosives, pauses; 9% of its energy in 2–4.5 kHz and 2% above 4.5 kHz, in line with
long-term speech spectra). Two talker kinds: one close talker, and café babble (6 talkers). Room echo by distance,
set as direct-to-reverberant ratio with RT60 0.45 s: 30 cm (+12 dB), 1 m (+5 dB), 2 m (0 dB). Chirp at −34 dBFS at the
mic, mic self-noise −66 dBFS. "Speech level" is speech RMS relative to the chirp RMS at the mic (+12 dB = speech
four times louder). Each cell is the **worst** of the two talker kinds. "pair ≤15 s" is the chance that at least one
packet decodes during one 15 s broadcast, for a listener that starts at a random moment.

**Not modelled:** speaker and microphone frequency response (laptop speakers can roll off above 6–8 kHz), clock
drift between devices, other noise types. Confirm the winners on hardware before shipping (docs/NEARBY.md §4).
